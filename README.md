# ⚔️ Mortal Shell II – Official Boss & Region Tracker

An interactive, dark fantasy Soulslike checklist and lore codex for **Mortal Shell II** (Release: August 20, 2026).

Structured strictly in accordance with official **Game8 & Rock Paper Shotgun Guides** and cross-referenced with the **Unreal Engine 5 Game Files (`G:\Games\Sparta`)** across all **39 in-game boss encounters** in 9 distinct regions.

🎮 **Live GitHub Pages**: [https://entchen66.github.io/mortal-shell-ii-bos-tracker/](https://entchen66.github.io/mortal-shell-ii-bos-tracker/)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **39 Official In-Game Boss Encounters in 9 Progressive Regions**:
  1. **Disciple's Pass & Fallgrim (Prologue)**: Gloombound Ritualist, Tainted Vestige, Tar Golem, Great Arbiter of Flesh
  2. **Fainweald & Sunken Village**: Vrannic the Grand Illusionist, Circle of the Grasping Root, The Wandering Shepherd, Magdalena
  3. **Blackridge Pass & Sanguine Shoreline**: Broodshell Swarm, Grisha, Lucian the Thirsting Knight, Tarblighted Stoner, The Lost Child
  4. **Gloomshade Grove & Citadel of Penance**: Bloodcursed Lithopod, Rabid Grisha Pack, The Warden, Pale Grisha
  5. **Prisoners' Domain & Executioner's Pulpit**: Vrago, Gloombound Legion, Tishina's Confessor, The Nameless Captive
  6. **Ruins of Mammon & Obsidianite Mines**: Infested Miner, Vellen, Twin Sesters & Sester Secundus, Bor the Deluded Monarch, Sariel the Endless, Gloombound Wraith, Starved Harridan, Prophet of Profane Infinities
  7. **Withered Shoals & Conquered Temple**: Hexapod, Strange Grisha, Droeg the Conquerer, Urrig the Executioner & Caged Grisha
  8. **Faded Citadel & Obsidian Vault**: Sir Isaac the Scholar-Prince, Solnir the Gloom Seeker, Monolith & Malborn Offspring, Orrem the Reclaimed
  9. **The Final Crucible & The Primordial Ovum**: Zmey the Unbidden (Dark Father), The Ascendant Echo (Secret True Ending Trial)

- **100% Genuine In-Game Screenshots (Zero AI Images)**:
  - Official screenshots directly from Game8 and Rock Paper Shotgun.
- **Atmospheric Soulslike Melodic BGM**:
  - Procedural Web Audio API acoustic lute / harp melody in D Minor over ethereal cathedral strings with a 140Hz high-pass filter (no muddy bass).
- **Bilingual Support (German / English)**:
  - High-res vector SVG flags for instant language switching.
- **Interactive Boss Codex & Filtering**:
  - Detailed modal view with weakness guides, shell recommendations, lore quotes, fast travel navigation, and local storage state persistence.
